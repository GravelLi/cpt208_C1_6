Requirements List

Three must-have conditions for a playful HerRun experience

These placeholders define the minimum conditions the system should meet to feel approachable, socially engaging, and emotionally rewarding.

Must-Have 1

Encourage low-pressure engagement

The experience should help students join without feeling judged, overcommitted, or overwhelmed too early.

Must-Have 2

Support playful social interaction

The system should make communication, participation, and shared progress feel light, friendly, and motivating.

Must-Have 3

Provide emotionally positive feedback

The platform should reward effort with visible progress, encouragement, and small moments of delight.
