Requirements List

Three must-have conditions for a playful HerRun experience

These placeholders define the necessary conditions the system should meet to feel approachable, socially engaging, and emotionally rewarding.

Must-Have 1
😊

Mood-based Mission Generation

The system adjusts the day’s mission based on the user’s current mood, recovery state, and cycle-related condition, this makes the experience feel more personal, achievable, and encouraging for users who want a lighter and more emotionally supportive way to stay active.

Must-Have 2
⭐

Progress Visualisation and Reward mechanism

By completing gentle tasks or even choosing rest when needed, users can earn stars such as Calm Stars or Rest Stars, which gradually unlock supportive features like night run audio, a better plan for tomorrow, and a period-friendly run plan. This makes progress feel playful and rewarding without relying on competition or performance rankings.

Must-Have 3
💬

Emotionally Affirming Feedback Language

Instead of focusing only on performance or completion, the system responds to users with gentle encouragement, rewarding feedback, and emotional reassurance, helping them build a more positive and sustainable relationship with exercise.
