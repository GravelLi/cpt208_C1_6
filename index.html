About the project

What are we building?

Our project explores a digital system for a university running club. The goal is to create a platform that supports both everyday runners and organizers. Runners need a simple way to discover events, join sessions, and stay engaged. Administrators need a more efficient way to manage schedules, announcements, attendance, and communication.
