Human-Centric Computing Group Portfolio

A modern running platform for connection, safety, and organization

HerRun is a university HCI portfolio about designing a running experience that feels safer, more welcoming, and more playful for student communities, especially women runners balancing wellbeing, social confidence, and everyday campus life.

Overview

What this portfolio is trying to show

This website is structured as a true multi-page portfolio so each major part of the project can stand on its own. Instead of forcing everything into one long scroll, each page focuses on one phase of the HerRun process and keeps the story clearer for review.

Project Direction

Human-centered and community-driven

HerRun focuses on women runners, student wellbeing, group coordination, playful motivation, and a stronger sense of trust before and during participation.

Portfolio Logic

From problem framing to reflection

Each page shows one major stage of the design process with meaningful placeholders so the site already feels complete and ready for later final project content.

Visual Identity

Soft, editorial, and consistent

The portfolio keeps the HerRun pink/red palette, rounded card system, soft shadows, and polished feminine aesthetic across every page.
