Final Reflection

Social implications, ethics, and AI-supported process notes

This text block is designed for the final written reflection that closes the portfolio with broader insight.

HerRun raises questions that go beyond interface polish. Designing for women runners in a university environment means thinking carefully about safety, inclusion, emotional confidence, and the social signals that affect whether someone feels welcome enough to participate.

The project also creates space to reflect on how playful interaction can support wellbeing without trivializing legitimate concerns around trust, visibility, and coordination. This section will later document how AI tools were used to support ideation, drafting, and prototyping while keeping human judgment central to research interpretation and ethical reflection.
